## Included on the website
- About Me introduction and professional portrait
- MECG, Mount Meru, ESG Lab, Cateena Partners, Distributed Energy, and SWE / Phi Sigma Rho experiences
- Specific Michigan Engineering competency reflections for each experience
- Selected projects and outcomes
- Twelve-competency self-assessment and two SMART goals
