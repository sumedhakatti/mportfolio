2. Why did you rank your first choice #1?

I chose Global Business/Operations because I enjoy using engineering and business skills to improve how companies work. It also connects to my interest in consulting and operations.

3. Brainstorm 2–3 Honors Capstone ideas

1. Help a manufacturing company reduce costs and improve its supply chain.

2. Develop a data-based system to improve how a business schedules staff or manages customers.

3. Study how a company can reduce energy use while keeping operations efficient.

I would enjoy a project with a real organization where I can use IOE tools and communicate useful recommendations.

4. Three possible focus-area themes (including one custom theme)

Business / Global Operations: Connecting engineering with strategy and company operations.

Entrepreneurship: Learning how to develop and evaluate new business ideas.

Custom: Data-Driven Business Decisions: Combining analytics, finance, and operations.

5. Three SMART goals for exploring the focus area or capstone

1. By December 2026, meet with an Honors advisor to discuss my three focus-area options and identify at least three eligible courses.

2. By February 2027, speak with two professors or professionals about potential operations or consulting capstone projects.

3. By March 2027, compare at least three project ideas and prepare a short proposal for my preferred focus area and capstone direction.
