02 / EXPERIENCE

Learning by doing.

Each experience has helped me develop a different set of Michigan Engineering competencies. These reflections connect what I did to how my skills grew.

Michigan Engineering Consulting Group

Vice President of Consulting

Expanded our client portfolio by bringing in General Motors and Intuit, two Fortune 500 companies, and helped diversify the work across eight projects this semester. Our projects included a commodity trading and risk management system (CTRM), an AI financial auditor and summarizer, and work involving water filtration, real estate, and AI scheduling. I also introduced a project manager bootcamp to help students prepare for leadership roles.

Engineering competencies developed

Leadership: Creating the PM bootcamp taught me to develop other leaders rather than only managing projects myself. It also helped increase student interest in leadership opportunities.

Communication: Bringing in new corporate clients required explaining the team's capabilities and connecting client needs with project scopes. I learned to communicate differently with company representatives, project managers, and student consultants.

Systems Thinking: Working across finance, AI, water, and scheduling projects helped me see how technologies, workflows, and business requirements must fit together for a solution to work.

Mount Meru Group · Zimbabwe

Operations & Cost Optimization Intern

Worked on factory-line automation costs and reduced the proposed cost by 57%. I also created an accounts payable dashboard that automated the workflow from collecting invoices and extracting information through organizing data for Odoo.

Engineering competencies developed

Systems Thinking: Building the accounts payable workflow taught me to see invoice collection, data processing, and ERP entry as connected steps. I had to consider how changes in one step affected the entire process.

Creativity: Reducing the automation cost required looking for lower-cost ways to achieve the factory's objectives instead of simply accepting the original approach.

Risk Management: Working on financial data and automation taught me why accuracy, consistency, and process checks matter when replacing manual work with automated systems.

ESG Real Estate Laboratory · Jan–Jun 2026

World Cup Business Development Intern

Researched ten FIFA World Cup host-city markets and assessed more than fifteen possible partnership opportunities to support business development, sponsorship, and activation planning.

Engineering competencies developed

Entrepreneurial Mindset: Comparing markets and possible partnerships helped me learn to look for business opportunities when information is incomplete and options have different strengths.

Risk Management: Evaluating multiple opportunities taught me to compare potential benefits and uncertainties before recommending where to focus further research.

Communication: Organizing research into clear findings helped me practice making complex market information useful for business discussions.

Cateena Partners · Dubai

Private Equity Intern

Worked on investment screening, company and industry research, financial valuation analysis, and presentations for potential investments.

Engineering competencies developed

Risk Management: Evaluating businesses taught me to examine financial assumptions, consider what could go wrong, and avoid relying on a single number when assessing an investment.

Communication: Preparing research and investment presentations helped me turn financial analysis into findings that other people could understand and use.

Ethics: Handling investment research reinforced the importance of using reliable information, presenting limitations clearly, and not overstating conclusions.

Distributed Energy · Pune

Pre-Sales Intern

Reviewed multiple energy-policy documents, supported financial models, and helped prepare project proposals and presentations that considered investment returns and project feasibility.

Engineering competencies developed

Lifelong Learning: Reading unfamiliar policy documents helped me practice learning technical rules independently and understanding how they affect energy projects.

Systems Thinking: Connecting policy requirements with financial models and project proposals showed me how regulation, engineering, and business decisions influence one another.

Communication: Working on proposals helped me explain technical and financial information in a format useful to prospective clients.

Society of Women Engineers · Phi Sigma Rho

Mentorship & Campus Community

Introduced a mentorship program through SWE to help students reconnect and build stronger relationships. I am also involved in Phi Sigma Rho, where I have had opportunities to participate in a supportive engineering community.

Engineering competencies developed

Empathy: Designing a mentorship initiative encouraged me to think about why students can feel disconnected and what support they may need to build relationships.

Leadership: Taking the initiative to start the SWE program taught me to turn a community need into a structured idea that others could participate in.

Teamwork: My involvement in Phi Sigma Rho has helped me appreciate the role of shared responsibility and peer support in a student community.
